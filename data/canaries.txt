# and again by cmd/verify against the compiled rule-sets and PAC file in CI.
# A mismatch fails the build, so a bad upstream update cannot silently block a
# major site or send it the wrong way. Format: <domain or IP> <block|direct|proxy>
# Domains are not resolved (like no-resolve rules), so IP-only behavior such as
# GEOIP is tested with IP entries.
